BreakBox Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-22

BreakBox (package stonehead.breakbox.main, "the app") is a game developed by an individual. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information we collect

Information collected by the developer: none. The app has no server of its own, no sign-up, login or payments, and does not use location, contacts, photos or similar permissions.

Information stored on your device only (never sent to the developer or any third party)

  • The name you enter for the ranking (up to 15 characters) and your score
  • Your stone balance and unlocked items

All of it is removed when you uninstall the app or choose "Settings > Apps > BreakBox > Storage > Clear data".

Information collected by the advertising SDK (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads. The SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • IP address (may be used to estimate the general location of a device)
  • User product interactions (app launch, taps, video views)
  • Diagnostic information (app and SDK performance such as launch time, hang rate, energy usage)
  • Device and account identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID, and similar)

See Google's disclosure, the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

2. Purposes

  • Serving ads and measuring ad performance
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Analytics of app and SDK performance

The developer does not access this data and uses no analytics or crash-reporting tools of their own.

3. Sharing with third parties

Ad-related information is sent to Google LLC (AdMob). It is not shared with any other third party. Transmission is encrypted with TLS.

In regions that require consent (EEA, UK and others), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent screen is shown on first launch, and ads are not requested without consent.

4. Retention and deletion

  • In-app data: stored only on your device and deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Ad data: retained by Google under its policies. The developer cannot delete it, but you can limit or delete it as described in section 5.
  • Send access or deletion requests to the contact below.

5. Opting out of personalized ads

  • Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads): "Delete advertising ID" or turn off ad personalization
  • Google Ad Settings: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Where the consent screen appears (EEA, UK and others) you can change your choice at any time in the app under "Help > Ad privacy settings".

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (recommended for ages 13 and up) and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that a child's information was collected, we will delete it.

7. Changes

When this policy changes, this document and its effective date are updated.
